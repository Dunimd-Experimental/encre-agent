## 2. Legal Framework and Regulator

We process personal data under the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023 ("NDPA"), which replaced the NDPR 2019 framework (now applied as subsidiary guidance).

| Role | Detail |
|---|---|
| Regulator | Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) |
| Website | ndpc.gov.ng |
| Complaints | through the Commission's channels |

Given our processing connected to users in Nigeria we maintain a designated contact for data protection matters reachable at dunimd@outlook.com.

## 3. What We Collect

3.1 **Local-first:** Encre Agent keeps agent definitions, prompts, responses and history on your device by default.

3.2 Telemetry is off by default; prompts/responses are not stored server-side unless a cloud feature requires it.

3.3 Cloud services process: account details (email), authentication records, service metadata (timestamps, error codes), billing records for paid plans.

## 5. Cross-Border Transfers

Transfers outside Nigeria occur only where the NDPA requirements are met: adequacy or appropriate safeguards (standard contractual clauses, binding corporate rules) or statutory exceptions including your consent. Details in [DATA_PROCESSING.md](DATA_PROCESSING.md).

## 6. Your Rights

Under the NDPA you may access, correct, delete, object to processing, withdraw consent and lodge complaints with the NDPC. Write to dunimd@outlook.com; we respond within approximately 30 days.

## 7. Security and Breach Notification

7.1 Measures: TLS 1.2+ in transit, AES-256-GCM at rest, least-privilege access, confidentiality undertakings.

7.2 **Breach response:** where a breach is likely to result in a risk to data subjects' rights, we notify the NDPC within **72 hours** of becoming aware and inform affected individuals without undue delay; internal incident records are kept.

## 8. Artificial Intelligence

Nigeria has no comprehensive AI statute at this time; we follow NDPC guidance and voluntary good practice: transparency about AI interactions, labelling of synthetic media, human review of consequential automated decisions on request, and no training on user content without consent.

## 9. Changes and Contact

Material changes are announced at least 30 days before they take effect. Contact: dunimd@outlook.com · dunimd.com.
