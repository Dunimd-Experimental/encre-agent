## 3. What We Collect

3.1 **Local-first:** Encre Agent keeps agent definitions, prompts, responses and history on your device by default.

3.2 Telemetry is off by default; prompts/responses are not stored server-side unless a cloud feature requires it.

3.3 Cloud services process: account details (email), authentication records, service metadata (timestamps, error codes), billing records for paid plans.

## 4. Purposes and Legal Grounds

Under section 30 we rely on:

| Purpose | Ground |
|---|---|
| Providing the service | contract / consent |
| Security and abuse prevention | legitimate interests |
| Legal compliance | legal obligation |
| Optional telemetry | express opt-in consent |

4.2 We never use your content for model training without your express consent.

## 5. International Transfers

Transfers outside Kenya occur only under sections 48–49: appropriate safeguards (contractual commitments aligned with recognised international frameworks) or statutory exceptions such as your consent. Details in [DATA_PROCESSING.md](DATA_PROCESSING.md).

## 6. Your Rights

Under section 26 you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, object to processing or withdraw consent. Write to dunimd@outlook.com; we respond within approximately 30 days. You may also complain to the ODPC.

## 7. Security and Breach Notification

7.1 Measures: TLS 1.2+ in transit, AES-256-GCM at rest, least-privilege access, confidentiality undertakings.

7.2 **Breach response:** where a breach is likely to result in a risk to data subjects' rights, we notify the ODPC within **72 hours** of becoming aware (section 43) and inform affected individuals without undue delay; internal incident records are kept.

## 8. Artificial Intelligence

Kenya has no comprehensive AI statute at this time; we follow ODPC guidance and voluntary good practice: transparency about AI interactions, labelling of synthetic media, human review of consequential automated decisions on request, and no training on user content without consent.

## 9. Changes and Contact

Material changes are announced at least 30 days before they take effect. Contact: dunimd@outlook.com · dunimd.com.
