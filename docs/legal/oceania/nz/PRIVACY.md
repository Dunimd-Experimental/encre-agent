## 3. Information we collect (local-first)

Encre Agent's architecture is local-first:

- Agent definitions, prompts, responses and history stay on your device;
- Telemetry is off by default — diagnostics are sent only after you explicitly opt in;
- Prompts and responses are not stored by default;
- Server-side processing occurs only when you voluntarily activate cloud services.

| Category | Examples | Processing |
|---|---|---|
| Account data | Email address, display name | Cloud services only; account provisioning |
| Payment metadata | Order ID, amount | Handled by payment provider |
| Cloud inference content | Prompts/responses (PiscesLx only) | Processed in memory; metadata max. 30 days |
| Telemetry | Error reports, performance data | Opt-in; max. 90 days |

## 4. Purposes of collection, use and disclosure

We collect personal information only where necessary for a lawful purpose connected with our activities (IPP 1) and by lawful, fair, minimally intrusive means (IPP 4):

- providing, operating and securing the services;
- account management and billing;
- security, fraud prevention and abuse defence;
- further purposes only with your explicit consent.

We never use your content for model training without your explicit consent.

## 5. Compliance with the information privacy principles (IPPs)

- **IPP 1–4 (collection):** purpose limitation, source transparency, awareness at collection, fair manner.
- **IPP 5 (storage and security):** technical and organisational safeguards — see section 7.
- **IPP 6–7 (access and correction):** see section 6.
- **IPP 8–10 (accuracy and use):** reasonable accuracy checks before use; use limited to the notified purposes.
- **IPP 9 (retention):** minimised storage periods — deletion cycle table in the [Data Processing Overview](DATA_PROCESSING.md).
- **IPP 11 (limits on disclosure):** disclosure only within permitted purposes.
- **IPP 13 (unique identifiers):** we neither assign nor require unique identifiers from other agencies.

## 6. Your rights

You may request:

- access to your personal information (IPP 6);
- correction (IPP 7);
- erasure, to the extent not required by retention duties;
- information about automated decisions.

Send requests to dunimd@outlook.com — we usually respond within about **20 working days**. If unsatisfied, you may complain to the Privacy Commissioner (privacy.org.nz).

## 7. Security and privacy breaches

7.1 Technical and organisational measures: transport encryption (TLS 1.2+), encryption at rest (AES-256-GCM), access restrictions and confidentiality undertakings.

7.2 **Notified privacy breaches:** we assess every suspected incident promptly and, once we believe it has caused or is likely to cause serious harm, notify the Privacy Commissioner and affected individuals as soon as practicable (Privacy Act 2020, Part 6). All incidents are recorded in an internal register.

## 8. Disclosure outside New Zealand

Under IPP 12 we transfer personal information offshore only to recipients who provide comparable safeguards to those under the Privacy Act — e.g. via appropriate contractual guarantees or on adequacy grounds. Details in the [Data Processing Overview](DATA_PROCESSING.md), section 5.

## 9. Artificial intelligence

9.1 New Zealand has no comprehensive AI statute; we follow transparency standards voluntarily.

9.2 We disclose when users interact with AI systems; synthetic media (deepfakes) must be labelled as such.

9.3 Automated decisions: on request we explain the logic involved and provide human review.

9.4 No model training on user content without explicit consent.
