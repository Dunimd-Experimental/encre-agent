**Last updated: August 22, 2026**

It addresses federal privacy law (*Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act*, S.C. 2000, c. 5 — "PIPEDA") and Quebec's *Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector* (CQLR c. P-39.1, as modernized by Law 25). This is the sole applicable version for Canadian users.

## 2. Legal framework

| Instrument | Key points | Regulator |
|---|---|---|
| **PIPEDA** (Schedule 1 principles) | Accountability; identifying purposes; consent; limiting collection/use/disclosure; accuracy; safeguards; openness; individual access; challenges | Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (OPC) |
| **Quebec P-39.1** (Law 25 modernization) | Confidentiality by default; privacy officer designated (default: highest-ranking officer); incident registers; privacy impact assessments; portability (s. 27); transparency for decisions based exclusively on automated processing (s. 12.1); de-indexing/republication right (s. 28.1) | Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec (CAI) |
| Alberta PIPA / BC PIPA | Equivalent provincial regimes apply to activities within those provinces | OIPC Alberta / BC |

Proposed federal reform (Bill C-27 / CPPA) was not enacted and does not form part of this policy.

## 3. Information we collect

3.1 **Local-first architecture.** Agent definitions, prompts, responses, conversation history, and integrations remain on your device. Telemetry is off by default. Prompts and responses are not stored by default.

3.2 Only when you enable optional cloud services do we process: account data (email, authentication), inference request metadata, and optional telemetry you explicitly enable.

## 4. Purposes and limits on use

4.1 We collect only what is necessary for providing, securing, and improving the requested service (PIPEDA Principle 4.4).

4.2 We do **not** use your content to train models without your explicit consent.

4.3 We do not sell personal information.

## 5. Consent

5.1 Consent is obtained meaningfully: we explain purposes at or before collection, and you may withdraw consent at any time, subject to legal and contractual restrictions and reasonable notice (Principle 4.3).

5.2 Sensitive information is collected only with express consent.

## 6. Your rights

6.1 Under PIPEDA: access to your personal information, correction, and complaints — first to us, then to the OPC.

6.2 Under Quebec Law 25 additionally:

- access and rectification;
- de-indexing of material published unlawfully, or cessation of republication (s. 28.1);
- data portability in a structured, commonly used technological format (s. 27);
- information about decisions made exclusively on the basis of automated processing, the logic involved, and the main consequences, plus observations from you (s. 12.1);
- a simple, free complaint process to our privacy officer.

6.3 Requests: **dunimd@outlook.com**. We respond within **30 days** (access/correction deadlines under PIPEDA s. 8(3) and Quebec s. 39).

## 7. Children

Canadian law sets no statutory self-registration age; we nonetheless require **parental consent under 13** (see [Minors' Privacy](MINORS_PRIVACY.md)) and apply heightened protection to minors' data.

## 8. Security and breach response

8.1 Safeguards include TLS 1.2+ in transit and AES-256-GCM at rest, least-privilege access, and staff confidentiality obligations.

8.2 If a breach creates a **real risk of significant harm (RROSH)**:

- we report to the **OPC** as soon as feasible (PIPEDA ss. 10.1(1), Breach of Security Safeguard Regulations, SOR/2018-64);
- we notify affected individuals as soon as feasible, directly where possible;
- for Quebec incidents creating a risk of injury, we notify the **CAI** and affected persons as quickly as possible (s. 3.5);
- we keep a register of **all** breaches for **24 months**, regardless of severity.

## 9. Transfers outside Canada

9.1 Personal information transferred to a processor abroad remains protected under PIPEDA's accountability principle; contractual safeguards apply.

9.2 For Quebec residents: before communicating personal information outside Quebec, we conduct and document a **privacy impact assessment**, considering sensitivity, purposes, protection regime of the receiving jurisdiction, and contract terms (s. 17).

9.3 Where EEA/UK/Swiss law applies to specific flows, SCCs/IDTA or adequacy instruments are used (see our EU/UK/CH documents).
