## 3. Information We Collect

3.1 **Local-first architecture.** Encre Agent stores agent definitions, prompts, responses, and chat history on your device. We do not receive this data unless you enable an optional cloud service.

3.2 **Telemetry is opt-in.** No diagnostics are sent until you explicitly enable them.

3.3 **Account data** (cloud services only): email address, authentication identifiers, billing metadata.

3.4 **Service metadata:** timestamps, token counts, and error codes from optional PiscesLx inference, retained at most 30 days.

## 4. Purposes of Processing

We process personal information to: provide and secure the services you request; authenticate accounts; process payments; send service communications; comply with law; and protect against fraud and abuse.

**We do not use your prompts, responses, or content to train AI models without your explicit, opt-in consent.**

## 5. Sale, Sharing, and Disclosure

5.1 We do **not** sell personal information and do not "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising within the meaning of the CCPA/CPRA.

5.2 We disclose personal information only to service providers bound by contractual restrictions (e.g., payment processors), to regulators where required by law, and in connection with a corporate transaction under protective terms.

5.3 Sensitive personal information (if any) is used solely for the purposes you request and as permitted by state laws.

## 6. Your Privacy Rights

6.1 **California (CCPA/CPRA):** right to know/access, delete, correct, port; right to limit use and disclosure of sensitive personal information; right to opt out of sale/sharing (we engage in neither); non-discrimination for exercising rights.

6.2 **Other states (VA, CO, CT, UT, TX, and the states listed in §2):** rights to confirm/access, correct, delete, port, opt out of targeted advertising/sale/profiling, and appeal adverse decisions.

6.3 **Procedure:** email dunimd@outlook.com. We verify requests and respond within **45 days**, extendable once by a further 45 days where reasonably necessary. You may use an authorized agent with written permission. Appeals: reply to our decision email or write to dunimd@outlook.com (state appeal laws provide timelines, typically 45 days).

6.4 **Colorado AI Act:** for consumer-facing AI interactions we disclose that you are interacting with an AI system; if an adverse decision concerning you were made via a high-risk AI system, you could request its basis and human review. Encre Agent is a general-purpose tool and is not deployed by us for consequential decisions.

## 7. Children's Privacy

7.1 We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent (COPPA). See [Minors' Privacy](MINORS_PRIVACY.md).

7.2 Teens aged 13–17: under California law, we do not sell personal information of consumers we know are under 16 absent affirmative authorization; similar opt-in regimes apply in other states.

7.3 California's Age-Appropriate Design Code is currently enjoined in litigation (NetChoice v. Bonta); we nonetheless apply child-protective design defaults voluntarily.

## 8. Security and Breach Notification

8.1 Security measures: TLS 1.2+ in transit; AES-256-GCM at rest; least-privilege access; see [Data Processing Overview](DATA_PROCESSING.md) §6 and our public security policy (SECURITY.md in the repository root).

8.2 Breach notification: all 50 states, D.C., and territories require notification without unreasonable delay; typical statutory outer limits range from 30 to 60 days (e.g., Colorado, Florida 30 days; most states "expedient"/45 days; California requires notification "in the most expedient time possible"). We notify affected individuals and, where thresholds are met, state attorneys general and consumer reporting agencies within the strictest applicable timeline.

## 9. Cross-Border Transfers

9.1 Primary processing occurs in the United States.

9.2 Consistent with EO 14117 and 28 C.F.R. Part 202, we do not conduct covered data transactions involving bulk U.S. sensitive personal data with countries of concern.

9.3 Where EU/EEA/UK users interact with our services, transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914) or the UK IDTA; see the EU and UK notices in this repository.
