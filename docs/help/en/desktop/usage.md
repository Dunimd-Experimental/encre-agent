Usage stats help you understand model consumption and your habits. View them under **Settings → Usage**.

## Overall metrics

The top of the page shows core cumulative numbers:

- **Total sessions**
- **Total tokens** (input / output)
- **Tool calls**

## Multiple views

- **By model / by session**: usage distribution across models or sessions
- **Daily usage** (stacked): day-by-day consumption trend
- **Token trend**: input/output tokens over time
- **By mode**: General vs Automation consumption

## Activity heatmap

The "activity heatmap" shows your usage consistency:

- Active days and streaks
- Peak hours (intensity per hour)
- Peak periods

![Usage stats](/screenshots/usage-panel.png)

## What it's good for

- See which model costs the most and adjust your defaults accordingly
- When the token trend spikes, check whether automation tasks are running heavily (see [Automation](/en/desktop/automation))
- Pair with [Models](/en/desktop/models) to assign the right model per task

> [!NOTE]
> Stats are local and update when you import/export data.
